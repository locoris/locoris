# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Locoris community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

The maintainer will communicate moderation decisions when appropriate and will
respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Locoris community spaces and when an
individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Until a dedicated project
mailbox is available, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting
form and prefix the report title with `[Conduct]`. Reports are visible only to
the maintainer and will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Enforcement follows the four impact levels described by Contributor Covenant:
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban. The response will be
proportionate to the impact, intent, history, and risk of continued harm.
